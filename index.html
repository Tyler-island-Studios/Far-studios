What’s on

FÃR CINEMA 2027 · January 14–30

FÃR CINEMA 2027 is a special 17-day exhibition at FÃR Studios in Chiang Mai, bringing together emerging and established artists from Thailand and around the world. From January 14 to 30, the studio fills with independent film screenings, a photography exhibition, live performance, artist talks and hands-on workshops.

Inspired by travel, exploration and discovery, it’s a meeting point for artists, filmmakers and the local community, with opening and closing celebrations, special events and free general admission every day.

Rules for entrants

FÃR CINEMA 2027

  • Who can enter: artists anywhere in the world. Work can be from any year.
  • Entries: no limit. Each entry pays its own fee.
  • Under 18: a parent or guardian must give consent.
  • Content: all work must comply with Thai law.
  • Fees: nonrefundable, and paying doesn’t guarantee selection.
  • Copyright: you keep it. Selected work may appear in the online catalog and on our media platforms.
  • Recognition: selected artists get a certificate and festival laurels. No cash prizes in 2027.
  • Unsold prints: shipped back at your cost, or kept by FÃR Studios or destroyed.
  • Travel: artists pay their own travel, accommodation and visas.
  • Photo files: if selected, send full-resolution TIFF (preferred) or JPEG when requested.
