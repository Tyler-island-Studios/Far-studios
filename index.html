What’s on

FÃR CINEMA 2027

FÃR CINEMA 2027 is a special 17-day exhibition at FÃR STUDIOS in Chiang Mai, bringing together emerging and established artists from Thailand and around the world. From January 14 to 30, the studio fills with independent film screenings, a photography exhibition, live performance, artist talks and hands-on workshops.

Inspired by travel, exploration and discovery, it’s a meeting point for artists, filmmakers and the local community, with opening and closing celebrations, special events and free general admission every day.

Independent and experimental programs, from micro shorts to features.

Photography

FÃR STUDIOS

The studio

FÃR is a creative studio, artist residency and gallery space.

We bring together global artists working across fine art, film, photography, performance and new media.

Our story

Founded on Nantucket. Found globally. Go FÃR.

FÃR STUDIOS was founded by Tyler Island, an interdisciplinary artist and filmmaker from Nantucket, often called the Faraway Island.

A former whaling capital and international seaport, the island has long been a hub for artists and creatives. FÃR STUDIOS is inspired by that same spirit of exploration, adventure and discovery through the arts.

What we do

Gallery & exhibitions

We curate and present exhibitions of local and international artists across visual art, film, photography, performance and new media.

Artist residencies

Time, space and support for artists to develop new work in Chiang Mai. About the residency →

Creative studio

As a contemporary art practice and studio, we make our own work and produce collaborative projects with artists and partners.

Workshops & events

We run workshops, screenings, artist talks and festivals like FÃR CINEMA, open to artists and the public.

Global partnerships

We work with partners worldwide, with a focus on the Global South, and currently support projects in Thailand and the Caribbean.

Design & build

We design and build for exhibitions, events, creative projects, and bespoke experiences from concept to finished space.
